Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

What this policy covers

This policy applies to prepaid AI generation credits for the ConvertShorts Creative Studio, bought as one-time top-ups of ₹100, ₹500 or ₹1,000 through Razorpay. All quick tools and Creative Studio editors are free and involve no payment.

No refunds on purchased credits

Credits are a digital balance that is added to your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. Once credits have been added, purchases are final and are not refunded, including for unused credits. Unused credits do not expire, so you can use them at any time.

Failed generations

Each generation reserves credits before it is sent to the AI provider. If a generation fails, the reserved credits are returned to your balance automatically. If a generation completes, its credits are used even if you are not happy with the result, because the provider has already charged for it. The price of each generation is shown before you submit it.

Payment problems

If you were charged but credits were not added to your account, or you were charged more than once for the same top-up, email us within 7 days of the payment with your account email and the Razorpay payment ID. After checking the payment we will either add the missing credits or refund the incorrect charge to your original payment method. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days; your bank may take longer to show them.

Cancellation

There are no subscriptions and nothing renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. You can stop using the service at any time. A top-up cannot be cancelled after payment is completed.

Delivery

Credits are delivered digitally to your ConvertShorts account, normally within a few minutes of a successful payment. Nothing is shipped.
