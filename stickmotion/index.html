A SMALL STUDIO FOR BIG IDEAS

Make your first animation.

  1. Choose your cast. Add a character and pick its colour.
  2. Add actions. Select Walk, Wave, Jump or another action. Clips play in order for each character; different characters act together.
  3. Shape the movement. Click a timeline clip. Set its length and start/end position. Drag the character on the canvas to move it.
  4. Make a custom action. Add Custom pose. Edit its start pose, then its end pose: drag the coloured joints. Add more custom clips for complex sequences.
  5. Tell the story. Add props and timed captions. Props can move between two positions. Choose a background and video size.
  6. Export. Press Export video. The animation records in real time as a silent WebM video. Keep this tab visible until it finishes.

This is a hands-on animation tool. It doesn't interpret unrestricted prompts or invent actions. You control the result through clips and poses.
