Purchases and AI generation are not open yet. We are configuring Razorpay and testing the generation backend. All three top-ups provide the same supported AI capabilities after launch; prices depend on model, resolution, output count and duration. They do not include unlimited generation. Our generation price is reviewed provider cost + 20%, converted to INR, with the final amount shown before submission. 1 ConvertShorts credit = ₹1, including fractional credits. A ₹1,000 top-up gives 1,000 credits; a ₹200 provider cost uses 240 credits after our markup.

What every AI balance will include

All three options will use the same initial AI models: FLUX Schnell and Nano Banana 2 images, Nano Banana 2 editing, Kling 2.6 text/image-to-video, ACE-Step and MiniMax Music 2. Choose a model, set its supported parameters and review the exact INR price before submission.

The launch prices use reviewed provider cost + 20%, followed by daily USD/INR conversion and rounding up once to the next paise. For example, a $0.35 five-second silent Kling clip becomes $0.42 before INR conversion. A five-second clip with generated audio costs $0.70 at the provider and $0.84 before INR conversion.

Confirmed failed AI requests return reserved balance. Purchases will use verified Razorpay checkout. 3D/world generation, AI video VFX, speech and sound effects are planned separately; they are not included in the initial AI launch.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

Can I use ConvertShorts without an API key?

Yes. Customers do not need a provider account or API key. Free local editors are available now. After the AI launch, sign in and use your prepaid ConvertShorts balance.

Are AI generation and paid plans available now?

They are coming soon. We are configuring payments and testing the backend. The purchase buttons are disabled until the supported generation and payment flow is ready. You can explore the tools and create an account today.

What do the ₹100, ₹500 and ₹1,000 plans include?

They are one-time top-ups for the same supported AI capabilities: images and image editing, text/image-to-video, and music. Every option includes all free local tools. The amount is a generation balance, not a fixed number of videos or an unlimited subscription.

Which AI models are planned for the initial launch?

FLUX Schnell and Nano Banana 2 for images, Nano Banana 2 for image edits, Kling 2.6 for text/image-to-video, and ACE-Step and MiniMax Music 2 for music. Other models, 3D/world generation, AI video VFX, speech and sound effects are planned separately.

How long can I access my saved work?

Signed-in accounts have a 500 MB cloud media allowance. Each cloud file and synced project is available for 30 days from first save, then expires and is removed by scheduled cleanup. Download important work or a backup before the displayed deadline. Your account and unused AI balance remain available.

How do credits and the 20% markup work?

1 ConvertShorts credit equals ₹1. A ₹1,000 top-up gives 1,000 credits. If a generation costs ₹200 at the provider, the 20% markup makes it 240 credits (₹240), leaving 760 credits. Fractional credits are supported. The markup is applied to generation cost, not again to top-ups.

Do plans expire or renew automatically?

There is no automatic renewal or monthly subscription. Unused prepaid generation balance does not expire. The separate 30-day limit applies to cloud files and synced projects.

Can I control lyrics, singers and video duration?

Song composers show lyrics and style. ACE-Step supports requested length; MiniMax chooses its output length. Vocal preference and excluded styles are prompt guidance, not guaranteed singer or filtering controls. Kling 2.6 supports 5 or 10 second clips and optional generated audio.

Does ConvertShorts include every ArtCraft feature?

ConvertShorts integrates seven licensed browser editors and its own canvas, media, scene, library and project workflows. It does not include every ArtCraft native or private AI service. Suno, Midjourney, SwitchX relighting and some desktop-only workflows are not part of the current service.
