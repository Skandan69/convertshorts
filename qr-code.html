One QR works across UPI apps

A UPI payment QR created here contains the recipient’s UPI ID, display name and optional payment amount. A payer can scan it using a compatible UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM. The payer should confirm the recipient name shown by their app before authorising payment.

Personal and business payment QRs

Anyone with an active UPI ID can create a personal payment QR. Businesses should normally use the verified merchant QR supplied by their bank or payment provider when they need merchant features, settlements, soundbox support, refunds or transaction reporting.
