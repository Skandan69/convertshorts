Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

The short version

Your files never reach us. Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your own browser, so the videos, PDFs, images and documents you convert stay on your device from start to finish. We have no server that receives them, no storage where they sit, and no way to see them.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details, because nothing here requires them.

What we do collect

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which tools they use, and roughly where in the world they are. This tells us which tools to improve. It records things like pages viewed, time on page, approximate region, browser and device type. It does not record the contents of your files.

Cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell returning visitors from new ones. You can block or delete these in your browser settings and every tool on this site will keep working normally.

Advertising

We may display ads supplied by Google AdSense. When we do, the following applies.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

What we never do

We do not sell data. We do not upload, copy, scan or retain your files. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves. We do not share information with anyone beyond the analytics and advertising providers named above.

Hosting

This site is served as static files by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request information such as IP address for delivery and security. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no account data, there is generally nothing personal of yours for us to export or delete. If you have a question about data handling, email skandan.kovuri@gmail.com or use our Contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
