Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

The short version

The free quick tools and editors run in your browser: files you open in them stay on your device. If you create an account, save to the cloud library, generate with AI or buy credits, we and the services listed below process the data needed to do that. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

ConvertShorts (convertshorts.com) is operated by [Seller legal name], [Postal address with PIN code], India. Phone: [Support phone number]. Email: skandan.kovuri@gmail.com. In these pages, "we" and "us" mean ConvertShorts and its operator.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth), plus sign-in timestamps.
  • Cloud library: files, projects and settings you choose to save, with their names and sizes.
  • AI generation: your prompts, settings, and any reference images or audio you attach, the resulting media, and a record of each request (model, status, credits used, time).
  • Payments: order and payment identifiers, amounts, dates and status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's checkout and are never received or stored by us.
  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, approximate region, browser and device type, through Google Analytics, and standard request logs (including IP address) kept by our hosting providers for security.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

Why we use it

To provide your account, library and AI generations; to take payments, keep your credit balance and meet tax and accounting duties; to prevent fraud and abuse; to answer support requests; and to understand which tools to improve. We use your email to send account messages (such as confirmation and password reset), not marketing, unless you ask us to.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: accounts, database and cloud storage (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • fal.ai and the AI model providers it hosts: receive your prompts and reference files to generate results (fal.ai is a US company; processing is mainly outside India).
  • Razorpay: payment processing in India.
  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery (global network).
  • Google: Analytics and AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to measure visits and, where ads are shown, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We share only what each needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

  • Cloud files and synced projects: deleted automatically 30 days after first save.
  • Generated media held for you in the studio: under the same 30-day library rule. Providers may keep request data for a short period under their own policies.
  • Account data: until you delete your account or ask us to.
  • Payment and credit records: kept for up to 8 years for tax and accounting law, even after an account is deleted (without your login details).

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Email skandan.kovuri@gmail.com. Our grievance officer is [Grievance officer name] ([Support phone number]). If we cannot resolve your complaint you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Accounts, cloud storage and paid AI generation are for people aged 18 or over, or used with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly create accounts for children without that consent; contact us and we will delete such an account.

Cookies

Sign-in keeps a session token in your browser's local storage. Google Analytics and AdSense set cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; the quick tools keep working, but you will need to sign in again.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Database access rules keep each account's library, balance and orders private to that account. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at skandan.kovuri@gmail.com if you find a problem.
