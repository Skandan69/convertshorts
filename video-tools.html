How to trim, resize and compress a video

  1. Choose a playable video

    Add an MP4, WebM or browser-playable MOV file. Check the preview to make sure your browser can read the video.

  2. Trim and reframe

    Set Start and End in seconds. Choose a size preset or enter dimensions. Fit whole video keeps the complete frame with black bars when needed; Crop to fill removes excess edges.

  3. Set quality and export

    Choose MP4 or WebM and a quality setting. Optionally enter an approximate target size in MB. Export, keep the tab open while processing, and download the result.

Reduce video size for attachments and upload limits

A shorter duration and a lower bitrate can reduce video file size. Trim unnecessary seconds first. Then try Balanced or Smaller file quality, or enter the receiving service’s limit in Target file size. Use a little room below a strict limit because the target is an estimate.

For example, to aim below a 10 MB cap, you could try a 9 MB target and inspect the downloaded size. The result depends on duration, motion, detail and audio. There is no fixed compression percentage. If the picture looks too soft, increase the target or shorten the clip rather than repeatedly compressing an already compressed result.

MP4, WebM and browser processing

MP4 export uses H.264; WebM uses VP9. Input support depends on your browser’s ability to play the file, so a MOV extension alone does not guarantee compatibility. Re-encoding can affect quality even when you only trim a clip.

The input limit is 250 MB. Practical limits also depend on device memory, clip length and resolution. The first export downloads the video processing engine, but your media contents stay on your device. Keep this tab open; large jobs can take several minutes, especially on phones. Start with a short clip if you are unsure about your device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compress a video without losing quality?

This tool re-encodes the video, so quality can change. Try High quality first and inspect fine detail and movement in the result. A very small target for a long video is likely to reduce visible quality.

Will the output match my target MB exactly?

No. The target uses an estimated bitrate, and the final size can differ. Download and check the file, then adjust the target or trim more if you need to meet a strict limit.

Can I trim an MP4 without installing software?

Yes. Choose the MP4, set Start and End in seconds, and export. Processing happens in the browser. The result is re-encoded rather than a lossless stream copy.

What is the difference between cropping and resizing?

Resizing changes the output width and height. Cropping removes edges to fill that frame. Fit whole video preserves the complete picture and adds black bars when its proportions differ from the output.

Can I compress videos on a phone?

The interface adapts to smaller screens, but export requires a compatible browser and enough memory. Short clips are more practical on phones. If export fails, try a smaller output size or a desktop browser.

Is video compression free and private?

Yes. No account or watermark is added, and media processing is local. Internet access is needed to load the tool and its processing engine. Download your result before leaving the page.
