Connect prompts, images and video

Start with a cinematic, product showcase or three-shot template. Connect matching ports on the canvas, or choose each step’s inputs from its settings. Use List view on a phone. The free guided prompt builder helps you describe subjects, camera movement, style and lighting.

Review the budget before running

Workflow generation will use the same initial image and video models as the studio: FLUX Schnell, Nano Banana 2 generation/editing and Kling 2.6. Each step produces one output. Set resolution, aspect ratio, five- or ten-second video duration and optional generated audio. Prices include our 20% markup, with 1 credit = ₹1.

The full estimate adds the individual generation prices. Each submitted step reserves its own credits. Independent steps run with at most two jobs together; dependent video steps wait for their images. A confirmed failure stops later steps. Pausing prevents future submissions while accepted jobs continue. Resuming reuses submitted jobs and completed outputs.

Keep the workflow and its results

Save workflows in your workspace and find generated images and videos in My Library. Export a portable ZIP with the workflow and reference media, or import it as a new editable workflow. Generated files can be downloaded before their cloud copies expire.
