About ConvertShorts

ConvertShorts is a small collection of free tools that run entirely inside your web browser.

Why it exists

It started with a frustration. Converting a landscape video into the vertical format that Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok require should be simple, but nearly every free tool made it painful: sign up, upload the file, wait, then discover a watermark stamped across the result or a paywall in the way.

The technology to do that work in the browser already exists. So we built it that way instead, and kept adding tools that had the same problem.

How it works

Every tool here runs as JavaScript and WebAssembly on your own machine. When you drop in a video, your browser does the encoding. When you convert a PDF, your browser reads and rewrites it. Nothing is transmitted to a server at any point.

That has three consequences worth knowing. It is fast, because there is no upload or download wait. It is private, because your files never leave your device. And it is genuinely free, because we pay no bandwidth or processing costs for the work your own computer does.

What's here

Video Converter — convert between 16:9 and 9:16 with segment-by-segment crop and zoom control.

PDF & File Converter — PDF to Word or images, images to PDF, and spreadsheet formats.

Stickman Animator and Stickman Studio — stick figure animation with action clips, custom poses and timelines.

Infinite Canvas — an endless whiteboard for mind maps and diagrams.

Art Studio — draw, colour in and animate frame by frame.

Limitations we're upfront about

Browser-based processing has real trade-offs. Very large files depend on your device's memory. PDF to Word extracts text rather than rebuilding the original layout. PowerPoint conversion is not offered at all, because doing it properly needs a server-side office engine — which would mean uploading your file, and that is the one thing this site does not do.
